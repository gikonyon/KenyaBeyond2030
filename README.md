# 🇰🇪 Kenya Beyond 2030: Devolved Industrial Co-Reliance Dashboard

An interactive econometric and policy platform built with Streamlit to analyze public finance absorption, subnational productive capacity, and devolved industrialization across Kenya's 47 counties.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

This dashboard synthesizes public expenditure panel data from the **Office of the Controller of Budget (CoB)**, **KNBS**, **CRA**, and **National Treasury** to model county development budget execution and evaluate the economic footprint of **County Aggregation and Industrial Parks (CAIPs)** across **9 Functional Regional Blocs**.

### 🌟 Key Features
- **Executive Summary:** Macro metrics across all 47 counties and productive-capacity mapping.
- **County Industrial Matrix:** Deep-dive tabular register of local industry, infrastructure, jobs, and capital options.
- **Regional Specialization:** Grouped economic profiles across functional regional blocs (LREB, NOREB, CKEB, JKP, FCDC, etc.).
- **Budget Absorption Simulator:** Interactive econometric scenario tool modeling capital absorption based on Own-Source Revenue (OSR), Recurrent Spend share, and Pending Bills.
- **Constitutional Guardrails Register:** Legal verification tracking Articles 96, 118, 209, and 212 of the Constitution of Kenya (2010).
